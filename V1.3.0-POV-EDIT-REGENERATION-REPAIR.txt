OpalReader v1.3.0 — POV edit / regeneration repair

- Existing generated audio remains playable after a POV/narrator edit.
- Regenerate with current cast remains visible whenever prior chapter audio exists, including when that audio is marked stale by a POV edit.
- Stale audio is treated as the safe fallback until replacement generation succeeds.
- Successful replacement generation continues to swap in the newly generated audio only when ready.
- Repairs chapters already left in the stale-audio state by earlier POV edits without requiring deletion or re-import.
