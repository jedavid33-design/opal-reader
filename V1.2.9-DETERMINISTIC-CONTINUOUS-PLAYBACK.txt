OpalReader v1.2.9 — Deterministic continuous chapter playback

Fixes chapters that sometimes fell back to segmented playback even after generation was complete.

Changes:
- Any fully generated chapter now prefers composite playback even when resuming from a later narration segment.
- When resuming mid-chapter, Reader requests a continuous composite of the remaining segments and seeks within the first segment using the saved position.
- If a chapter finishes generating while segmented playback is already in progress, the next segment boundary switches into a continuous composite for the remainder of the chapter.
- Player label shows “continuous chapter” or “continuous from segment X/Y”.
- Progressive segmented playback remains available while a chapter is still generating.
- No TTS regeneration is required. Existing R2 segment audio is reused.
