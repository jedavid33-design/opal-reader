## The correction

**Old sentence (Addendum 1, §B1, new §3 paragraph, second
sentence — the clause following "The exclusion above is a
*dispatch* exclusion:"):**

> the retired plan can never run again, and no occurrence is
> ever granted under two plans.

**Replacement (Rowan's suggested wording, used verbatim;
it replaces that clause in full, so the paragraph now opens
"The exclusion above is a *dispatch* exclusion." followed by
these sentences):**

> The retired plan is fenced from any further dispatch or
> grant claim. Its existing historical grant rows remain
> attached to the old job and cannot authorize the new job.
> A newly approved plan can grant a previously attempted
> occurrence again, which is precisely why position 50's
> possible repeat Google charge is disclosed below.

**Why.** The retired plan's job already retains historical
`granted` rows for the 18 occurrences (plan §3, item (iii),
unchanged), and the proposed fresh job will use *new* grants
for some of those same occurrences, including position 50 —
so "no occurrence is ever granted under two plans" is false
across history. The claim the records support is the one the
replacement states: no further valid dispatch or grant claim
under the retired plan, historical grant rows that cannot
authorize the new job, and no simultaneous dispatch from two
active plans. The correction preserves — in fact sharpens —
Addendum 1's central distinction between **dispatch safety**
(which the fence provides) and **repeat provider charging**
(which no fence can exclude, and which §B1's position-50
disclosure addresses).
