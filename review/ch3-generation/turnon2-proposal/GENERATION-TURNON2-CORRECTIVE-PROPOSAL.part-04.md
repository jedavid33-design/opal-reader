### 6.4 Stand-down, and what happens to the GO

What it does to the GO is a **proposal, not a decree** — Rowan rules, Julie adopts (OQ-6): the new gate's GO passage be drafted with a **validity window**: a GO consumed by a pre-act stand-down (fault, clearance failure, or the §3-A-variant no-submission expiry) **remains live for exactly one re-run of the same gate within 72 hours** of the stand-down, once clearance is re-established under §6.2–6.3; a second stand-down, or 72 hours elapsed, lapses it, and the next attempt needs a fresh GO.
