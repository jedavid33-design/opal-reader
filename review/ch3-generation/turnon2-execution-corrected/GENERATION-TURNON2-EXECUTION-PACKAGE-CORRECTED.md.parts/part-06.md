### §4.10 The WAIT law — deterministic deadlines (A1, OQ-1)

**Definitions.** PT-day reset = **07:00Z daily** (midnight PT; the guard's day boundary). For a smoke plan created at **T0**:

- **Inner bound** = **T0 + 4h**.
- **Outer bound** = **(first PT-day reset strictly after T0) + 4h**.
- **Extension deadline** = **outer bound + 4h** — reachable only if the extension is **earned** (below).
- **Absolute cap** = **T0 + 52h**. No classification lands later than the cap, under any branch.

**Disposition of the extension deadline (R2).** The corrective proposal's Option C text carried the extension further than this package does: its extension was "one further window (**second reset + 4 hours**)" (proposal §3-C; OQ-1 as recommended there). The uncorrected filing of this package adopted the shorter **outer bound + 4h** without expressly dispositioning the change. Rowan's review of this package (handoff `RT-H-20261107-cc21c8`) **accepts the shorter deadline as a conservative design amendment**, and this corrected filing records that disposition expressly: the extension deadline is **outer bound + 4h**; the **T0 + 52h absolute cap is preserved unchanged**; and every formula and worked example in this package — §3's schedule table and the worked example below included — is stated on, and has been verified consistent with, the outer + 4h basis. No deadline formula changed under this correction; the disposition is what was missing, and it is now of record.

**WAIT entry — evaluated once, at the inner-bound read.** If at the inner bound the plan is **not complete** and its **only** obstruction is a `parked_quota` / `provider_attempt_budget` park with **failed = 0**, no entry failed or blocked, and every other §4.11 COMPLETE-evidence item either satisfied or still satisfiable by the plan's resumption, the plan enters the named state **WAIT** instead of landing FAILED. WAIT entry is recorded in the working log with the inner-bound read's full values: the plan's totals — of which the **done count is the extension predicate's sole baseline** — and, recorded alongside as **diagnostic evidence only**, that read's dispatches and cumulative attempt/execution counts (R1: recorded, never predicate-bearing). If the plan is incomplete at the inner bound for **any other reason**, WAIT does not open and the classification lands at the inner bound (§4.11).

**During WAIT.** The system remains enabled under this section's authority alone (§4.12's no-indefinite-enabled predicate names WAIT as one of its enumerated states). Observation is **hourly** (§4.7), returning to ~5-minute polls at the reset and on any movement. The guard releases the park at the reset; the chain's standing registration resumes the plan on the fresh day — **no wake act is authorized in WAIT either** (OQ-4). All triggers, the unavailable-source law (§4.12), and the fault path (§4.11) run unchanged. The stall test stays suspended for the quota park only.

**The extension — earned only by completed-segment progress (R1).** At the **outer-bound read**, compare the plan's completed-segment count against the inner-bound read's recorded baseline: the extension is **earned** iff **done_outer > done_inner** — and by nothing else. An increase in the plan's cumulative attempt or execution count **without** an increase in completed segments is **insufficient and never earns the extension**; attempt and execution movement between the two reads is recorded in the working log as **diagnostic evidence only** — it informs the record's account of the plan's behavior and moves no deadline. The done counts are read from the plan at both reads and recorded; the extension can be earned **at most once**. If earned and the plan is still incomplete at the outer-bound read — parked again on the fresh day's quota, or still progressing without having completed — the decision deadline moves to the **extension deadline**. If not earned, or the plan is incomplete at the extension deadline, the classification lands at that deadline (§4.11).

**Exit from WAIT.** The plan reaching its COMPLETE evidence set at any read inside the applicable deadline classifies COMPLETE **at that read** (COMPLETE is judged whenever its evidence first stands complete — it does not wait for a bound). Any non-quota stop state, any failed entry, any foreign movement, or any trigger event exits WAIT immediately into the ordinary classification/trigger path, judged on its own terms at that moment. A verified 1101 fault in WAIT suspends under §4.11's fault law — on verification + the exception window, not at a bound.

**Worked example (the planned morning schedule, §3).** Suppose Julie submits at **14:15Z** on gate morning (9:15 a.m. ET): **T0 = 14:15Z**. Inner bound = **18:15Z** (1:15 p.m. ET). The first PT reset strictly after T0 is the next morning's **07:00Z** (2:00 a.m. ET), so the outer bound = **11:00Z next day** (6:00 a.m. ET) — the park, if taken on gate day, releases at that 07:00Z reset and has four hours of fresh-day running before the outer read. If the plan re-parks on the fresh day but **done** rose between the inner-bound read and the outer-bound read — **done_outer > done_inner**, the sole earning condition (R1); attempt movement alone, however large, would not earn it — the extension deadline = **15:00Z next day** (10:00 a.m. ET). The absolute cap = **T0 + 52h = 18:15Z two days after T0** (1:15 p.m. ET) — in this schedule the extension deadline (T0 + 24h45m) binds long before the cap; the cap exists so that **no** configuration of this law — including a submission later in its window — can hold the system enabled past a fixed, pre-computable moment.
