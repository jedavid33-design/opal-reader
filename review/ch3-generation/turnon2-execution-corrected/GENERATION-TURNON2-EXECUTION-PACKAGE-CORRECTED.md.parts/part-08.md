### §4.13A The partial-lift contingency — PARTIAL-LIFT ABORT (R3)

**Entry conditions.** Act 1 (the halt lift, §4.6) has completed with **all** its required read-backs verified; Act 2 (the queue unpause) has then either **failed** — the PUT returned an error, or its read-back shows `delivery_paused` still true — or is **unverified** — the PUT's outcome or its read-back could not be obtained, so Act 2's effect is unknown. Entry into this branch is itself the first recorded event of the branch, and **Julie is notified as soon as the branch is entered**, with Act 2's observed state stated at exactly the strength the evidence supports.

**The smoke never opens in this branch.** There is no P-1 observation window and no A6 snapshot (both presuppose two verified lifts), no submission window, and no smoke. The gate's only remaining business is the safe re-hold, the notifications, and the record. The inherited plan is not released by anything in this branch beyond whatever Act 2 may have done before its failure was established — the halt having stood lifted only until the re-hold below — and it is left exactly as it stands.

**Determination reads (read-only; never counted).** Before any response act, the executor takes the branch's determination set: a fresh queue GET; `/settings`; `/health`. Their purpose is to establish Act 2's outcome class as precisely as the evidence allows — **failed**, **unverified**, or **applied-despite-the-failed-response** (the queue reads unpaused) — and the class is recorded with its evidence. If the determination reads cannot be completed, Act 2's class is **unverified** and the response proceeds at (c).

**The authorized response — the safe re-hold of Act 1, under the GO's standing authorization for this branch (§5).** (a) **Act 2 failed** (queue verified still paused): the sole response act is the **re-hold of Act 1** — the `GENERATION_HALT` binding flipped back to `"1"` by the same verified settings-envelope mechanism as this gate's other flips (the complete then-current envelope, all code parts byte-identical to the live build, every secret `inherit`, observability preserved) — with the **full read-back set** captured: `/settings` shows `GENERATION_HALT` = `"1"` with the same 10 bindings; `/health` reports generation `halted` and build `ef69b8f9…`; the script ETag is unchanged (`d0eb6ea9…`, against P-1's saved artifact); observability still enabled + persisted. (c) **Act 2 unverified:** the Act 1 re-hold runs anyway — the halt is the generation-controlling hold (dispatch is wired only when `GENERATION_HALT` is `"0"`), so a verified halt re-hold stops generation regardless of the queue's state — and the queue's state is carried as **unverified** in every report until a later read establishes it; a later establishing read is reported to Julie the same day it is taken.
