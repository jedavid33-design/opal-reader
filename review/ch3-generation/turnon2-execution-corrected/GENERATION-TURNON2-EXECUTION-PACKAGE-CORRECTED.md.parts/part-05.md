### §4.7 Observation discipline (all phases)

- **Recorder discipline (A-7, carried forward):** the drive-pass recorder is the observation spine. Every poll records the latest pass's identity and the values the classification will rely on; a recorder discrepancy of the R-06 class (a grant transition without a recorder row) is evaluated under T-4 at its stated rule — the transition's poll **plus one further complete poll** — never on a single read.
- **Poll cadence:** ~5 minutes during active phases (the P-1 window after the initial passes, the smoke's active generation, and around any PT reset or observed movement); hourly during WAIT (§4.10); the confirmation/census sets are single reads, not polls.
- **Exposure signals (report-only, carried forward; never limits, never triggers):** (i) the smoke plan's settled units exceeding **2× its census quote**; (ii) the smoke plan's dispatches exceeding **60 in a day**. Both are computed on the **smoke plan's own** accounting only — inherited-plan dispatches and settlement are a separate ledger (Appendix A) and never enter the smoke's signals.
- **The contemporaneous working log** is the gate's evidence spine from census to close; the execution record is written from it, and any claim in the record must trace to a logged read.
